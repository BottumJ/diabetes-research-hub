# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep criticism on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable here.

Two things specific to this project:

- **Corrections are welcome and are not an attack.** This hub publishes claims about medical research. Telling us a number is wrong, with the source, is the most useful contribution there is.
- **This is not a place for medical advice.** Please do not ask for or offer treatment recommendations in issues or pull requests.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainer, Justin Bottum, at justin.bottum@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential.
