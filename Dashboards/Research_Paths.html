How to Use This Dashboard

VALIDATED paths (10 total) are backed by 2+ independent published sources including systematic reviews or landmark RCTs. PARTIALLY_VALIDATED paths (2 total) have supporting evidence with important caveats or limited human data. UNVALIDATED paths (0 total) lack external confirmation despite corpus presence. What This Cannot Tell You

Validation was performed via targeted web search, not a systematic review. Some paths may have stronger or weaker evidence in the full literature than indicated here. This dashboard prioritizes paths extracted from high-quality papers, but absence of validation does not prove incorrectness—only that we could not confirm the finding through rapid evidence synthesis.

Limit of this badge, measured 2026-08-29. The tier is only as good as PubMed's publication-type field, and that field is assigned on a lag. 134 of 359 papers in this corpus (37%) carry no design-bearing publication type at all — 66% of 2026 papers and 46% of 2025 papers, against about 31% of papers published before 2018. The badge is therefore most reliable on the oldest evidence and least reliable on the newest, which is the opposite of what a reader would assume. Two concrete cases found the same day: PMID 36643381 is a trial protocol whose title says so and whose PubMed record does not, and PMID 36826844 is a JAMA randomised trial (CLVer, n=88) that PubMed has not tagged as one. See pubtype_title_disagreement.json.

Methodology

Research Path Extraction & Validation Pipeline

Stage 1: Corpus Analysis 91 full-text papers on diabetes complications were automatically extracted. Regex-based information extraction identified 472 key claims linking mechanisms (inflammation, oxidative stress, specific protein pathways) to outcomes (T1D, T2D, complications).

Stage 2: Path Clustering These 267 data points were grouped by mechanism-outcome relationships. Counts are computed at build time, not hardcoded. Identical matched text repeated within the same paper is collapsed to one data point (a dose restated 18 times is one dose, not 18 findings).

Stage 3: Validation Selection The top 25 paths (by data point frequency and clinical relevance) were selected for external validation.

Stage 4: Cross-Validation Each path was validated via targeted PubMed/Google Scholar searches for: (1) systematic reviews, (2) meta-analyses, (3) landmark RCTs confirming the relationship. Validation status was assigned as:

  • VALIDATED: 2+ independent high-quality sources (RCT, meta-analysis, systematic review)
  • PARTIALLY_VALIDATED: Supporting evidence with important caveats or limited human data
  • UNVALIDATED: No independent external confirmation found
  • CONTRADICTED: External evidence contradicts corpus findings (none found)

Note on Causality: This validation confirms observational and mechanistic correlations, not strict causality. Many paths show strong mechanistic evidence in preclinical models but modest clinical trial outcomes.
