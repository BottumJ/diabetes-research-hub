What This Dashboard Answers

This dashboard visualizes quantitative data that we actually extracted from 63 full-text papers using regex patterns and NLP-assisted identification. It answers: What quantitative data can we actually extract from our corpus? Which research gaps have the strongest corpus-backed evidence?

How to Use This

Each data point links back to its source PMID with surrounding context. Click any PMID to verify the claim in the original paper. Use this dashboard to validate claims made in other research dashboards and to identify which gaps have direct quantitative support in the published literature.

What This Cannot Tell You

Regex extraction is approximate. Numbers may come from different contexts (e.g., a C-peptide value from a control group, not a treatment group). Context snippets are truncated to 300 characters. Always verify the full context in the original paper before citing data. Missing papers in PubMed or non-English literature will not appear here.
